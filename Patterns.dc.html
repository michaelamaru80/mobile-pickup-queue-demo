Mobile patterns

Patterns agreed on the Mobile Pickup Queue. Each one is a live Untitled UI component (Draft, in the design system's mobile patterns) and follows the mobile-app-design skill. Reuse these before designing anything new.

Person card

UntitledMobile.PersonCard
  • Photo fills the left edge (120 pt), tap to enlarge; same-size “No photo” tile.
  • One full-width primary action; ⇄ move icon top-right (44 pt).
  • Student ID and mail code right under the name on the serve screen.

Numbered steps

UntitledMobile.StepHeader
  • Content aligns with the step title (44 pt indent); step buttons full card width.
  • Auto-scroll to the next step when one finishes.
  • Final action stays pressable at 50%; an early tap spotlights the missing step.

Grouped items

UntitledMobile.GroupBand
Shelf B · B9
Shelf A · A4
  • Full-width gray band per owner; label aligned with row text.
  • Used on the serve screen and in the scan sheet's list.
  • Proxy line under the name: “Picking up for …” in brand color.

Scan sheet

UntitledMobile.ScanSheet
  • Collapsed: progress, Done (disabled until a scan), “Can't scan a label?”.
  • Expand for To scan / Scanned; pencil to type, ✕ to remove with Undo.
  • All scanned: green bar, “All N scanned ✓”, Done pulses.

Camera fallback

UntitledMobile.CameraFallback
  • On every camera; white text on black (not a gray tertiary).
  • Search/check-in: back to the field with the keyboard. ID proof: “Type their ID” sheet, saved as weaker proof.
  • Frames 344 pt wide at a fixed top; close with ✕ only.

Move to a window

UntitledMobile.OptionRow

Cancel
  • Opened from ⇄ on the card; destinations with status dots.

  • Remind them to bring anything already pulled.

Dark toast

UntitledMobile.Toast tone="dark"
  • Screen confirmations are dark; the white pill is for camera views.
  • Action is a small text button (Undo, Try again), never a boxed button.
  • About 12 pt above the footer or tab bar.

Print to preset printer

UntitledMobile.PrintAction
  • No printer picker: sends to the printer set in settings.
  • After printing: “Print again” + a caption; the button never moves.
  • Receipts work the same from Completed.
